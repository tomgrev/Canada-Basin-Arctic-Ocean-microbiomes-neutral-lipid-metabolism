# Canada-Basin-Arctic-Ocean-microbiomes-neutral-lipid-metabolism
Metagenomic Discovery of Neutral Lipid Metabolism Pathways in the Arctic Ocean Microbiomes Suggests a Potential New Role in Survival and Oceanic Carbon Cycling - Scripts
Contains scripts that have been used for the analysis found in the paper: "Metagenomic Discovery of Neutral Lipid Metabolism Pathways in the Arctic Ocean Microbiomes Suggests a Potential New Role in Survival and Oceanic Carbon Cycling", T.Grevesse et al.

Descritpion of the files:
+ Grevesse_Canada-basin_Storage-compounds_scripts_building-KO-matrices.R : ensemble of R scripts designed at parsing metagenomic files retrieved from IMG/JGI, produce full genes annotation tables and compute KEGG orthologs abudnance per metagenomic sample
+ Grevesse_Canada-basin_Storage-compounds_scripts_Binning.txt : ensemble of scripts for binning metagenomic assemblies and quality control of resulting bins
+ Grevesse_Canada-basin_Storage-compounds_scripts_protein-taxonomy.txt : ensemble of scripts to retrieve the taxonomy of a selection of genes using DIAMOND and MEGAN
